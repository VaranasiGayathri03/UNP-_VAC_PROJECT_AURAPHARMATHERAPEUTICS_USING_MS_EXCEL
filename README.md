# UNP-_VAC_PROJECT_AURAPHARMATHERAPEUTICS_USING_MS_EXCEL
Aura Therapeutics – Pharma Distribution Analytics

Project Overview

A comprehensive data analytics project focused on pharmaceutical distribution, sales performance, revenue trends, prescription growth, marketing effectiveness, and territory-level performance. This project uses Excel-based data analytics techniques to transform raw pharmaceutical distribution data into meaningful business insights through data preprocessing, statistical analysis, data visualization, correlation, and regression.

Objectives

- Analyze pharmaceutical sales and distribution performance.
- Identify revenue trends across fiscal quarters, drug types, distribution channels, and therapeutic segments.
- Evaluate sales territory performance and quota achievement.
- Examine prescription growth before and after marketing campaigns.
- Investigate relationships between marketing expenditure, distribution reach, pricing, and revenue.
- Support data-driven business decisions through statistical methods and visualization.

Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Excel
- Data Cleaning & Preprocessing
- Statistical Analysis
- Pivot Tables
- Pivot Charts
- Correlation Analysis
- Linear Regression
- Hypothesis Testing

Data Preprocessing

The dataset underwent preprocessing to improve data quality and analytical reliability.

- Removal of duplicate shipment records.
- Identification and handling of missing values.
- Removal of irrelevant and redundant columns.
- Conversion of percentage-based text fields into numerical values.
- Binary encoding of categorical variables.
- Creation of numerical representations for categorical attributes.
- Feature engineering for prescription changes and other analytical variables.
- Preparation of a structured dataset for statistical analysis.

Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

The project explores pharmaceutical distribution performance through multiple dimensions:

- Quarterly revenue analysis.
- Drug-type revenue comparison.
- Distribution channel performance.
- Therapeutic segment analysis.
- Territory-level quota achievement.
- Prescription trends before and after marketing campaigns.
- Marketing expenditure and revenue relationships.

Data Visualization

Five pivot-style summary tables and five corresponding charts were created to visualize key business metrics.

1. Quarterly Revenue Analysis – Identifies seasonal revenue trends.
2. Revenue by Drug Type – Compares branded generic and pure generic performance.
3. Revenue by Distribution Channel – Evaluates revenue contribution across distribution channels.
4. Revenue by Therapeutic Segment – Identifies commercially significant therapeutic areas.
5. Quota Achievement by Territory Tier – Evaluates sales performance across territory classifications.

Statistical Analysis

Five statistical tests were conducted to evaluate business hypotheses:

Statistical Test| Purpose
Paired t-test| Evaluate prescription changes before and after a campaign
Independent samples t-test| Compare revenue between drug types
One-way ANOVA| Compare revenue across fiscal quarters
Chi-square test of independence| Examine the association between distribution channel and trade scheme activation
One-sample t-test| Evaluate quota achievement against a benchmark

Each test includes its hypothesis, test statistic, p-value, interpretation, and conclusion.

Correlation Analysis

Pearson correlation analysis was used to investigate the relationship between doctor detailing expenditure and quarterly billing revenue.

The analysis evaluates:

- Direction of relationship.
- Strength of linear association.
- Potential relationship between marketing expenditure and revenue performance.

Regression Analysis

Multiple linear regression was performed to examine the factors associated with quarterly billing revenue.

Independent Variables

- Doctor detailing expenditure.
- Realized strip price.
- Quarterly units distributed.
- Active chemist reach.
- Prescription change.
- Drug type.
- Warehouse hold days.

Dependent Variable

- Quarterly Billing Revenue.

The model evaluates regression coefficients, statistical significance, R-squared, adjusted R-squared, and overall model performance.

Key Business Insights

- Revenue varies across fiscal quarters, with stronger performance in Q2 and Q3.
- Branded generic products demonstrate higher average revenue than pure generic products in the analyzed dataset.
- Retail stockists contribute substantially to distribution revenue.
- Cardiology represents a major revenue-contributing therapeutic segment.
- Quota achievement is approximately 76.35%.
- Prescription counts show a substantial increase following the campaign period.
- Doctor detailing expenditure exhibits a moderate positive relationship with revenue.
- Units distributed and realized strip price are important revenue-associated variables in the regression model.

Business Recommendations

- Optimize inventory and marketing allocation based on quarterly revenue patterns.
- Evaluate product mix and branded generic performance.
- Strengthen high-performing distribution channels.
- Improve territory-level sales planning and quota monitoring.
- Assess marketing effectiveness using prescription growth, chemist reach, and revenue metrics.
- Use statistical findings to support strategic pharmaceutical distribution decisions.

Conclusion

This project demonstrates the application of data analytics techniques to pharmaceutical distribution and sales data. By integrating data preprocessing, exploratory analysis, statistical hypothesis testing, visualization, correlation, and regression, the project transforms raw data into actionable business insights. It highlights the importance of analytical decision-making in revenue optimization, marketing evaluation, distribution management, and sales performance monitoring.
